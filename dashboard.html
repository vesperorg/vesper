What this chain hides

Every payment lands at a one-time key, every amount is sealed in a commitment, and a payment never says which output it spends: it points at a ring of 16 outputs and proves it owns one. Nodes check that no coins were created by adding the sealed amounts together, never by reading them, and stop double spends with anonymous "spent markers". Only two numbers are public: the fee, and the mining reward. Your own balance is found by your wallet, scanning with your view key. A new output can be spent, or used as a decoy, once it is 10 blocks old.
